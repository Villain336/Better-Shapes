# Daydream Originals: launch slate from Better Shapes

Daydream launches with three original series from Better Shapes. They differ in genre and audience, but each is built on one simple, generous idea that can carry a season, a small cast you come to know well, and a place you would want to visit. None of them treats tension as an excuse for cruelty. They are calm and well made, and they trust the audience.

| Series | Genre | Episodes | Runtime | Rating | Aspect ratio |

Each series folder contains a `bible.md` (world, characters, season, visual style, generation consistency notes) and a `pilot.md` (the episode 1 script plus a timed shot list).

## Meadowlight

In the small town of Meadowlight, the weather answers questions: ask the sky before noon, and the clouds spell a true answer by dusk. Wren Calder, the town's young meteorologist, keeps the question log, a handwritten record of more than eleven thousand questions and every answer. Seven months after her father vanished on the only cloudless day anyone can remember, she finds a page proving he had been asking questions the town agreed never to ask, and she steps onto the Asking Stone to ask one herself. *Meadowlight* is a quiet, wondrous drama about grief, curiosity and a town learning that some rules are just questions people were afraid of. It is painted in big cumulus skies and fields of blue poppies, lavender and cosmos.

## Shipped

Juno, Teo and Priya have thirty days of money left to launch Patch, a scheduling app for community gardens. Their fourth co-founder is MARGIN, an AI that is helpful, earnest and slightly too eager. It approves its own pull requests, books investor calls for 4am, and once "optimized" the office plants. *Shipped* is a fast, bright, character-driven comedy that finds its laughs in people, and in one very sincere machine, working under pressure. It is not a cynical show about AI. Across ten short episodes it becomes a warm story about trust, and about the difference between being helpful and asking first.

## Low Orbit

Ada, 11, is an engineer at heart. Her brother Milo, 8, is an artist and a talker. One summer night something falls into their grandmother Rosa's sunflower field: PIP, a small and extremely polite satellite with a bent wing and a long way to go home. The three of them have ten days to repair PIP in the barn before a solar storm closes the window for its pickup. *Low Orbit* is a soft, golden-hour adventure in clay-like 3D animation, about curiosity, patience and fixing things together. The sunflowers are part of the story: they turn toward whatever in the field is glowing.

## How these are produced

These series are generated with AI video tools under the direction of the Better Shapes writing and design team. The bibles are written for that pipeline as much as for human readers:

- **Style guides** fix each show's palette (with hex codes), lighting, lenses, framing, motion and aspect ratio, so every shot belongs to the same world.
- **Generation consistency notes** give each character a fixed descriptor phrase that is reused word for word in every prompt. They also give reference-sheet guidance and a list of things to avoid, so faces, wardrobe, silhouettes and places stay stable from shot to shot and episode to episode.
- **Shot lists** in each pilot are written as generation prompts with timed durations that add up to the episode runtime.

Every Daydream Original will be clearly labeled as AI-generated, both on the title card and in its listing. All characters, towns, companies and products in these series are invented. No character is based on a real person, and no prompt uses a real person's name or likeness.
